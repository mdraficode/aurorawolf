## 2 · The six real defects found and fixed this session

1. **Bite-jam (invisible).** The engine's bite scan (`p3.attack()`) offers chunk
   `animals` + `predators` + rivals + legends and picks the **closest** inside a ±78° cone
   (`dot ≥ 0.2`). A deer between wolf and boss eats the strike silently — parklab17/18:
   2 counted bites, 0 boss damage. Three-part fix: `jam` sense in `EYES_FIGHT`
   (human.mjs), an **atomic strike-time re-check inside `H.bite()`** (aborts without
   burning the 0.72 s rate limit, retries next poll), and a `-grazers×2.5` penalty in the
   arena scorer (clearings = meadows = grazing land).
2. **The jam sense blocked on the boss itself.** Bosses live in `chunk.predators`
   (`p4.js:3144` `ch.predators.push(this)`) — the "jammer" at r 0.3 was the target.
   Both jam scans now skip `constructor.name === 'Boss'`.
3. **`holdN ≤ 2` rejected the dive's 3rd poll** — exactly the poll where the nose finally
   enters the ±78° bite cone (parklab26: `nv 1.04`, everything else green, hn 3). Now ≤ 3.
4. **Crouch stand-up fired before the bite** (parklab18) — every press was uncrouched.
   Stand-up now waits for the press (`fight.diveEnd` + atkCd/timeout check), and the
   fight-loop exit stands the wolf up. Full 7.5 press = (3 + 1 ambush + 1 crouch) × 1.5.
5. **Post-teleport geometry was backwards.** The ambush lands the **wolf at gap ≈ 0 —
   dead in the boss's FACE** (all labs: teleport → gap 0.1–0.6), not at its flank. The
   shut-in crossing now sprints when caught in-arc / fresh off a teleport (a walking cross
   is run down outside 4 m: boss walks 12.5 vs wolf 7).
6. **Speed-8 fights are unwinnable** (0.4 s decision batches: routepack2 = 0 bites, dead
   20 s, identical law that lands 9 presses at speed 2). Fix: **live boost switch** —
   `window.__boost.n` added to the boost loop in `index.html`; the router drops to
   `n = 2` on boss-stage entry and restores on exit (`mark('fight-speed')`, verified in
   routepack3/routehunt1). The cadence tuner tracks `liveSpeed`.

Also: **live report dump** — `mark(..., force)` snapshots `runs/run_<tag>_live.json`
(crashes at cap were losing whole-lab polls; parklab25/27/32 motivated this).

## 3 · The fight law now in `run.mjs` (strike-cycle grammar)

Faithful port of probe v24/v25 (the source documents its own physics) + two new ideas:

- **Windup** (`wind > 0 && ag < 1.52`): tangent WALK at r ≤ 3.6 (ω 2.7 vs the plant's
  0.4 neck → gap +2.3 rad/s through the windup, strike whiffs, 0 stam); sprint the
  tangent beyond 3.6 (a walk's ω = 7/r ≤ 1.9 cannot clear the 1.37 arc in 0.55 s).
- **Shut-in** (r > 3.95): crossing sprint rules per §2.5; stam floor 60 for luxury sprints.
- **Sprint leg** (post-strike, `b.atk ≥ 0.40`, wind gone, atkCd ≤ 0.1, stam > 18): tangent
  sprint θ1.50 to the tail. (Free-running it regardless of atkCd — lab37 — sprinted
  through the danger arc: 7 hits in 12 s. Reverted.)
- **Dive** (struckFresh && ag > 1.98): crouch (KeyX), 4-poll sequence thWant 0.55/0.90 in,
  2.20/2.60 out; press on the 2nd–3rd poll; stand-up deferred to after the press.
- **Park ring** (ag > 2.40 / 1.90): walk-tangent freeze, dead-behind, regen 11/s.
- **THE RESOLVE LAW (new)**: a bite lands **0.38 s after the press** (`atkT` windup) — its
  value is the geometry at RESOLUTION. Press gates now require
  `|gap| + gv·0.38 > 1.93` where `gv` is an EMA of the gap velocity — press early in a
  fast-growing sprint leg (resolves BEHIND, 4.5–7.5 dmg), never during the dive's inward
  collapse. This is the difference between lab35's 1-value presses and kills.
- Press value gates: flank-or-better only (`fm < 0.30` static gate superseded by the
  resolve law), jam-clear by +0.60 m, `holdN ≤ 3`, rate-limit free on jam abort.
