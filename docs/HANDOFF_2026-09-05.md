## 4 · Collision flake — RESOLVED (trainer's choice: fix the test, keep the game rule)

`test/collision.test.mjs` "sprint into the log stops at its bark" had become intermittent on
`a57eb5a` (2 of 3 runs failed). Diagnosis (measured with a 74-log sweep on seed 4242, both sides of
every log, on this build AND the previous one): the failures were **test-side**, and the same
sweep failed on the pre-`a57eb5a` build too — the standable rule only made the existing weakness
visible.
1. The test dropped the wolf from `heightAt + 0.5` instead of grounding it; on ground higher than
   the bark the new rule *correctly* lets the wolf step onto the log.
2. It measured the body against an ideal capsule, but the game's log is **five circles** (spacing
   1.6·s, radius 0.5·s + 0.55): a body wedged between two circles settles into a notch 0.27–0.55 m
   deeper than the capsule (tolerance was 0.22) — pure geometry, no penetration.
3. The trunk pick was random and the boulder lane unchecked — a second solid or water in the run-up
   stopped the wolf short of the wall (this was MASTER §11's "pre-existing load flake").

Fix (test only): grounded starts (`ground()` helper), deterministic nearest-first trunk with a
clear 9 m lane, clear-lane boulder placement, log assertions in the game's own terms (never inside a
circle, never across the axis, 40 walk stations across the covered span; stations where the bark is
at/below foot level are skipped as the standable rule). **10/10 consecutive passes**; `npm test` is
now 27/27 on this build.
