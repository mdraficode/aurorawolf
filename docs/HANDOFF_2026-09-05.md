## 4 · Known issue found while verifying (NOT fixed yet — needs a decision)

**`test/collision.test.mjs` — "sprint into the log stops at its bark" is now intermittent** on
`a57eb5a` (PASS/FAIL alternates across runs; 2 of 3 runs failed here). It was green before the
jumpable-logs commit, which added `so.top` to fallen-log solids and made `pushOutSolids` skip any
solid whose top is at/below the wolf's feet (`o.pos.y >= so.top − 0.05`). The test sprints the wolf
at a log from 9 m with `wolf.pos.y = heightAt + 0.5` (it never re-grounds before the run), so on
sloping ground the wolf's feet can sit above the log's 0.62 m top and it passes over/through the
capsule by design. Two ways to settle it — trainer's call:
1. **Test-side**: ground the wolf (`wolf.pos.y = heightAt(px0, pz0)`) before the run and assert the
   feet stay *below* the bark, i.e. update the assertion to the new jump/stand rules; or
2. **Game-side**: tighten the skip rule (e.g. require `o.grounded === false` or a real upward `vy`
   before ignoring a topped solid) if a running wolf should never pass a log without jumping.

The pre-existing "boulder" flake noted in MASTER §11 also surfaced once (at `2de0862`), so treat
`collision` as flaky until either fix is in.
